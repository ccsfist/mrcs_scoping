Conservation System

Land converted to conservation. Part of the farmer's land is converted to agroforestry. This is the farmer's main contribution to the program. The more land they convert, the less they grow in crops. Part of the community's land is dedicated to mangrove restoration. This is the community's main contribution to the program. The more land protected, the greater the buffer against storms and floods. Part of the pastoralist's rangeland is set aside for sustainable management. This is their main contribution to the program. Better managed land builds long-term productivity.

Conservation system income (good year). In a good year, the agroforestry system earns the farmer this much income. In a good year, healthy mangroves support strong fish populations and stable fishing income. In a good year, well managed rangeland supports healthy herds and strong income for the pastoralist.

Conservation system income (bad year). In a bad year, crops often fail but the forest keeps producing. This is how much income the farmer can still count on from their agroforestry land. In a bad year, healthy mangroves protect the household from storms and floods while keeping fish populations stable. This is how much the system still provides when a bad year hits. In a bad year, managed rangeland maintains herd productivity better than degraded land. This is how much income the pastoralist can still count on when a bad year hits.

Farmer income (good year). Fishing income (good year). Pastoralism income (good year). This is what the farmer earns from their crops in a good year. This is what the fisher earns from fishing in a good year. This is what the pastoralist earns from their herd in a good year.

Farmer income (bad year). Fishing income (bad year). Pastoralism income (bad year). This is what the farmer earns from their crops when a bad year hits. This is what the fisher earns from fishing when a bad year hits. This is what the pastoralist earns from their herd when a bad year hits.

Outside Labor & Income

Outside income. Some households earn extra income outside of farming. If this comes from the farmer's own work it strengthens their household. If it comes from a funder it is part of the program cost. Some households earn extra income outside of fishing. If this comes from the household's own work it strengthens their resilience. If it comes from a funder it is part of the program cost. Some households earn extra income outside of herding. If this comes from the pastoralist's own work it strengthens their household. If it comes from a funder it is part of the program cost.

AA Program

Bad year AA support. When a bad year is triggered, the household receives this amount as direct support from the program.

AA program cost. This is what it costs the funder to run the program, set as a percentage of the transfer amount.

Probabilities

Bad year probability. This is how often a bad year is expected to happen. You can update this based on local data. Communities in Manakara reported 6 out of 10 bad years over the past decade. Communities in Toliara II reported 5 out of 10 bad years over the past decade. Communities in Toliara I reported 7 out of 10 bad years over the past decade.

AA support failure rate. Sometimes the program does not trigger even in a bad year. This is how often that happens.
